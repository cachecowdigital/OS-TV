OS TV — How to open it

This app was sent to you directly rather than downloaded from the App Store,
so macOS will refuse to open it the first time with a message like:

    "OS TV" can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software.

That's just Apple being cautious about apps that aren't distributed through
their store — it's expected, and it's a one-time thing. To open it:

  1. Drag "OS TV.app" into your Applications folder (if you haven't already).
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) "OS TV.app" and choose "Open".
  3. A dialog will appear — click "Open" again to confirm.

After that first time, it opens normally with a regular double-click.

If step 2 doesn't show an "Open" option, or you already tried double-clicking
and got stuck on the warning:

  Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down to the
  "Security" section → click "Open Anyway" next to OS TV.

That's it — you only need to do this once.
